### 1. 籃球中鋒（D1）

A 6 ft 10 in (208 cm), 245 lb (111.1 kg) division I collegiate basketball center records the following preseason test results:
1RM back squat: 270 lb (122.5 kg) / 1RM power clean: 225 lb (102.1 kg) / Vertical jump: 18.0 in (45.7 cm) / Standing long jump: 112 in (284 cm) / V̇O₂max: 38 mL/kg/min

Which exercise should receive the greatest emphasis in the athlete's resistance training program?
1. Barbell back squat　2. Hang power clean　3. Loaded jump squats

> **Answer: Barbell back squat.** Rationale: Power scores are the most important for basketball, and his power results are above 60%. While to some of you the vertical jump may be low, research on D1 guards and forwards shows an average of about 17.1 inches, which would put 18 inches above 60% (additionally, this is a question where position is important). When looking at these test results without consulting the norm charts, an athlete who squats only 45 lbs more than his power clean suggests a strength issue. This value is around 53 percentile and makes back squat the right answer.

### 2. 女子曲棍球（field hockey）

A 160 lb (72.5 kg) female field hockey player has the following test results during preseason testing:
1RM bench press: 115 lb (52.2 kg) / 1RM back squat: 205 lb (93.0 kg) / Vertical jump: 18 in (45.7 cm) / 20 m sprint: 3.52 s / Pro-agility: 5.02 s

Which of the following exercises would be most appropriate to add to the subsequent training block?
1. Power clean　2. Back squat　3. Speed bench press

> **Answer: Power clean.** Rationale: Field hockey is a power and repeated sprint sport, so we must ensure that these test values are good before addressing anything else. We see that the vertical jump is good, but 20 m and pro agility are slow, showing a clear lack of acceleration and power. Reference: ESC5, Ch. 14, p. 338-366

### 4. 女子冰球（D3）

A 5'6 169 lb (76.7 kg) division III collegiate female hockey athlete has the following results from a test battery:
1RM Front Squat: 175 lb (79.4 kg) / 1RM Trap bar Deadlift: 225 lb (103 kg) / Vertical Jump: 17.5 in (44.5 cm) / Broad Jump: 58 in (148 cm) / 300-yard shuttle: 65 s / 1RM Bench Press: 115 lb (52 kg)

What changes should be made to the next training block?

> **Answer: focus on power development.** Rationale: This is a power, anaerobic, repeated sprint athlete, which puts the emphasis on power more than max strength. Specifically, her horizontal power is very poor, and that is very important in hockey.

### 5. 女子短距離游泳

A college junior female sprint swimmer receives the results of her first testing day. VO2 Max: 45 ml/kg/min, 1RM back squat: 115 lbs (55 kg), and a 1RM bench press: 98 lbs (44 kg). What changes would you make to the future program?
1. add a strength/power phase　2. add a max strength phase　3. add a hypertrophy phase

> **Answer: add a max strength phase.** Reference: ESC5, Ch. 14, p. 339. Rationale: A sprint swimmer is a power/strength athlete, with power being the most important, but there is no power test here. So the next most important quality would be strength, since there are only max strength tests, and this athlete is under the 60th percentile of strength, which would be the most appropriate change to the future program.

### 6. 男子籃球（測驗組合）

The results of a small battery of tests performed on a male collegiate basketball player weighing 230 lb (104.5 kg) 6 ft., 8 in. (203 cm) tall are as follows:
1RM back squat: 195 lb / Bench Press: 105 lb / Vertical Jump: 19.5 in / 20 yd dash: 3.4 sec / 5-10-5: 5.23 sec

Which of the following training factors is this athlete most in need of improving?
1. Maximal Strength　2. Anaerobic Capacity　3. Maximal Power

> **Answer: Maximal Power.** For this one, it comes down to a needs analysis for basketball. What is more important? Max strength or power? Does a max squat mean you can dunk or rebound? Power is the most important characteristic for basketball and should, therefore, be prioritized over max strength. While I agree we should work on max strength as a secondary emphasis in our program, it would not be the most important thing. Also, if a basketball player cannot jump at least 20 inches, there is a problem, and the other power measures are much too slow/low (20 yd and 5-10-5).
> All of these athletic profile questions will depend on the most important characteristic of the sport they play. Many sports rely primarily on power for performance (the ability to move a load fast is more important than the ability to move a heavy load slowly), so you would want to prioritize power. For a sport like basketball, the vertical jump is a critical performance factor and is a measure of power. For a sport like hockey (which is power dominant, but in the horizontal direction), the vertical jump would be a less important show of power than a power clean or a long jump.

### 8. 女子袋棍球（lacrosse）

A 61.5 kg female lacrosse player has the following test in her sophomore year of college
1RM bench press: 43 kg / 1RM back squat: 77.7 kg / Vertical jump: 14 inches / 20 m sprint: 4.5 sec / Pro agility: 6.0 sec

Which of the following exercises will help to improve the low scoring assessment?
A) power clean　B) deceleration drill　C) bench press

> She needs to develop power. From first glance, you should be very concerned with a 14-inch VJ, 6-second pro agility, and a 4.5 20 m as a coach. Her bench press could be improved, but for a lacrosse athlete, the most important variable tested in this question is power. Power = Work/time, so by increasing power, you can decrease the time taken to do the same amount of work, and in theory, working power cleans should improve VJ, 20 m, and Pro agility. While deceleration is important, she also needs to work on acceleration as well (shown in the slow 20 m and slow pro agility).

### 10. %1RM 表換算（10RM → 5RM）

An athlete completes a back squat test at 250 lb (114 kg) for 10 repetitions. The athlete will be training for 5 repetitions. What would the ideal weight be?
1. 285-290 lb (129.3-131.5 kg)　2. 295-300 lb (133.8-136 kg)　3. 280-285 lb (127-129.2 kg)

> **Answer: A.** Step 1: Find 1RM. 10RM = 75% of 1RM → 250 lb = .75 × 1RM → 1RM = 250/.75 = ~333 lb. Step 2: Find 5RM from 1RM. 5RM = 87% of 1RM = .87 × 333 lb = ~290 lb

### 26. 測驗順序

Which of the following tests should be performed first when conducted in the same test battery?
1. 1RM Snatch　2. T-test　3. vertical jump

> **Answer: Vertical Jump.** A non-fatiguing test should come first, and the vertical jump is considered part of this test category. Additionally, you can use logic and decide that the vertical jump is the least likely to impact future test results on this day when done first.

### 27. 評分者間信度

1. Low construct validity　2. Low discriminant validity　3. Low interrater reliability

> **Answer: Low interrater reliability.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 12. p. 252

### 28. 短距離蛙式測驗組合

When assessing the physical characteristics of competitive sprint breaststroke swimmers, a test battery should include which of the following?
a. vertical jump, 1RM back squat, 1RM bench press, 1RM pull-up
b. broad jump, 10RM leg extension, 10RM bench press, 10RM pull-up
c. vertical jump, 3RM power clean, 3RM bench pull, 10RM lateral raise

> The correct answer is A. A swim sprinter is a highly anaerobic athlete, and testing would look similar to a sprint track athlete; however, the bench and pull-up show the need for the power output of the upper body as well (not seen as much in track sprinters). Think of taking off from the start swim platform as a sideways vertical jump. To answer why it isn't C, that would reflect testing for a longer race (different energy system), and a 10RM is not specific to a swim sprinter.

## 五、課程設計

### 29. 需求分析：生理分析的構成

Which of the following is a component of the physiological analysis of a sport when designing a resistance training program?
1. training priorities　2. muscular involvement　3. muscle injury sites

> **Answer: training priorities.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 17, p. 441. Each needs analysis has 3 steps to it: movement analysis, physiological analysis, and injury analysis. In the physiological analysis, you must determine what physical characteristics are your training priorities (e.g., strength, power, hypertrophy, muscular endurance, and bioenergetic systems). This is different than what goes into the movement or injury analysis of the sport.

### 32. HIIT 休息區間

## 六、運動科學與營養

### 35. 第三類槓桿（投球前臂）

What class of lever is the lead forearm in a baseball pitch at ball release?
1. First class　2. Second Class　3. Third Class

> **Answer: Third class.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 2, p. 21; ESC5, Ch. 2, p. 28. Rationale: the fulcrum is the elbow; as it asks about the lead forearm, the muscle force is the tricep, which attaches very close to the fulcrum, and the resistive force is the ball. It is a third class because the moment arm of the resistive force is much longer than the moment arm of the muscle force. This is a good thing, as a third-class lever allows for greater speed at the cost of force output. The ball weighs very little, so moving it fast is more important than force output. I like to think of third-class levers as speed multipliers and second-class levers as force multipliers.

### 36. 第二類槓桿（力臂比較）

In which class of lever is the moment arm of the muscle larger than the moment arm of the resistance?
1. 1st Class　2. 2nd Class　3. 3rd Class

> **Answer: 2nd Class lever.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 2, p. 21

### 37. 高爾基腱器活化

Activation of the Golgi tendon organ involves which of the following?
1. force production within a muscle　2. contractile force of the tendon　3. rate of change in muscle length

> **Answer: force production within a muscle.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 1, p. 12. This is an example of reading the questions carefully. When the GTO is activated, what does it do? The GTO is responsible for inhibiting force production in a muscle to protect the muscle and tendon. A pneumonic I always teach is GTO sense tension (T for tension). Another important note is that the ability to override this inhibition at heavy loads is one of the most fundamental adaptations to heavy resistance training.

### 38. 內分泌系統適應（EXCEPT 題）

1. Hormone clearance time through the liver　2. Number of receptors in muscle tissue　3. Type of hormones released

> **Answer: Type of hormones released.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 4, p. 74.

### 39. 訓練後荷爾蒙反應的影響因素（EXCEPT 題）

An athlete just completed a resistance training workout. All of the following are factors that influence the magnitude of the post-workout hormonal response EXCEPT:

> **Answer: Amount of effort the athlete gives to maintain form during the workout.** Reference: ESC5, Ch. 4, p. 89

### 40. 主動恢復的效益

The performance of active recovery following an intense training session has shown the greatest impact on which of the following?
1. significantly reduced muscular fatigue　2. decreased blood lactate removal　3. improved psychological outcomes

> **Answer: improved psychological outcomes.** AR doesn't significantly improve muscular fatigue; the benefits are mostly derived from reduced perception of fatigue and soreness, which are psychological outcomes, not physical. Reference: ESC5, Ch. 21, p. 696 & Ortiz RO, Sinclair Elder AJ, Elder CL, Dawes JJ. JSCR 33(8): 2275-2287, 2019.

### 41. 支鏈胺基酸

Which amino acid has been shown to have the greatest effect on muscle protein synthesis?
1. Leucine　2. Valine　3. Isoleucine

> **Answer: Leucine.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 9, p. 183

### 42. B 群維生素角色

The inclusion of foods rich in B vitamins is recommended based on their ability to fulfill which of the following roles?
1. rate-limiting coenzyme for lipolysis　2. co-factor for protein synthesis　3. coenzyme for carbohydrate metabolism

> **Answer: coenzyme for carbohydrate metabolism.** Reference: ESC4, Ch. 9, p. 190

### 43. 多次腦震盪的性別差異

When a female athlete has multiple concussions, which of the following does she not experience?
1. greater changes between single-task gait activities　2. fewer changes between single-task gait activities　3. greater changes between dual-task gait activities

> **Answer: B.** "When multiple concussions occur, female athletes experience lower cerebral blood flow and greater changes between single- and dual-task gait activities compared to a single concussive event."
